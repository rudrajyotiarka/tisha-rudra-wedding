Love
that comes like fallen leaves in autumn.

Though wise men say only fools rush in,
He couldn't help falling in love with her. And honestly, it was a risk worth taking.

She, however, was wiser.
She weighed every possibility, analyzed every detail, and overthought every feeling.
Yet in the end, she found herself the exact same conclusion.

She loved him too.

Distance tested us. love answered.

Then came a year and a half of dates, memories, inside jokes, and collecting reasons to stay.

And when life decided to put oceans and time zones between us,
We learned.
That love isn't measured by the miles traveled together.
But by the commitment to keep choosing each other,
Through late-night calls, countdowns to reunions,
And far too many sad songs.

Turns out, distance can stretch a map, But it can't stretch two hearts apart.
